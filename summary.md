# Demand Forecaster — Sales & Inventory Prediction

## What Problem Does This Solve?
Businesses either overstock products (wasting cash on storage) or understock (losing customers and sales). This tool predicts future product demand so companies order exact inventory amounts ahead of time.

## How It Works (Simple Terms)
1. **Historical Data**: Analyzes daily sales data over the past year.
2. **Patterns Discovered**: Identifies overall growth trends, day-of-week demand spikes (e.g. weekend surges), and 7-day sales momentum.
3. **Future Prediction**: Forecasts exact units needed for upcoming days so stock matches real customer demand.

## 🗣️ How to Explain This in an Interview (30-Second Pitch)
> *"I created a demand forecasting tool that analyzes historical sales patterns, day-of-week trends, and recent demand momentum. It predicts future product demand so operations teams know exactly how much inventory to order, preventing stockouts and reducing holding costs."*
